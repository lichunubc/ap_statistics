Topic 2.2 Summary Statistics for Two Categorical Variables

A two-way table can answer different questions depending on which observations form the reference group. Before calculating any proportion, decide which observations belong in the denominator.

Count observations in both categories

Consider 24 café orders classified by the ordering channel and drink type. The observational unit is one order.

Channel Coffee Tea Smoothie Total
App 4 2 2 8
Counter 4 8 4 16
Total 8 10 6 24

An interior cell represents observations in two categories at once. App–Tea is 2. The margins are the row or column totals. Every observation contributes to exactly one interior cell, so both sets of margins total 24.

Joint, marginal, and conditional relative frequencies

Joint relative frequency is the proportion of all observations belonging to two categories simultaneously. For App and Tea, divide the interior cell by the grand total:

App and Tea among all orders=224=112≈8.3%. \text{App and Tea among all orders}=\frac{2}{24}=\frac1{12}\approx8.3\%.

Marginal relative frequency describes one category across the entire table. For Tea, divide its column total by the grand total:

Tea among all orders=1024=512≈41.7%. \text{Tea among all orders}=\frac{10}{24}=\frac5{12}\approx41.7\%.

Conditional relative frequency restricts attention to the group named after given or among. First choose that reference group, then divide by its total.

Tea among App orders=28=25%,App among Tea orders=210=20%. \begin{aligned} \text{Tea among App orders}&=\frac{2}{8}=25\%,\\ \text{App among Tea orders}&=\frac{2}{10}=20\%. \end{aligned}

The two conditional questions have the same numerator but different denominators. Reversing the condition can change the answer.

Why do the conditional denominators differ?

For Tea among App, only eight App orders are eligible; two are Tea, so the answer is 2/82/8. For App among Tea, there are ten eligible Tea orders; two came through App, so the answer is 2/102/10. The condition determines the reference group.

Joint App and Tea: 2 / 24 = 8.3%.

Reference values: App–Tea joint 2/24; Tea within App 2/8; App within Tea 2/10; Tea marginal 10/24; Tea within Counter 8/16; Counter within Coffee 4/8.

The notation P(Tea∣App)P(\text{Tea}\mid\text{App}) reads “probability of Tea given App.” The conditional probability rules are developed in Topic 2.6. For now, we are calculating relative frequencies directly from the table.

Compare conditional distributions

To compare drink mix by channel, use the total number in each channel as denominator:

Drink App (8 orders) Counter (16 orders)
Coffee 4/8=50%4/8=50\% 4/16=25%4/16=25\%
Tea 2/8=25%2/8=25\% 8/16=50%8/16=50\%
Smoothie 2/8=25%2/8=25\% 4/16=25%4/16=25\%
Total 100% 100%

Although both groups contain four Coffee orders, Coffee makes up 50% of App orders and 25% of Counter orders. A raw-count comparison would miss the unequal group sizes.

One can instead compare channel within a drink type by using column denominators. For example, the App share of Coffee orders is 4/8=50%4/8=50\%, while its share of Tea orders is 2/10=20%2/10=20\%. Those are legitimate answers, but to a different question.

Justify claims without asserting causality

If the conditional distributions differ, the variables show an association in the observed table. A defensible conclusion names both variables and gives comparable percentages:

In these 24 orders, Tea accounted for 50% of Counter orders but only 25% of App orders. Drink choices therefore differed by ordering channel in these records.

These descriptive data alone do not establish that using the App causes a Tea preference. They are not data from a randomized experiment. Also avoid generalizing the displayed proportions to all cafés without an appropriate sampling design.

A useful check: if each channel had the same within-channel percentages for each drink, there would be no association evident in this displayed table, even if the groups had different raw totals.

Check your reasoning

A classmate claims “Tea among App orders is 2/242/24.” Explain what 2/242/24 actually represents and correct the answer.

Check: 2/242/24 represents both App and Tea among all orders, a joint relative frequency. Tea among App orders uses the eight App orders as the reference group, so the correct conditional relative frequency is 2/8=25%2/8=25\%.
